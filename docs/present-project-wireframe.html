Core Concepts
▸ Enaction as gut feeling of where to go Core
Enaction: "gives rise to a gut feeling of where to go." Constructivism: "I know that from before and here is a little more." Additive/cognitive (constructivism) vs. orientational/whole-organism (enaction). Autopoiesis is the mechanism underneath -- the organism's continuous self-production determines what even registers as a perturbation worth responding to.

▸ Step Zero Core
If a student's autopoietic organization is running a threat response, the perturbations the teacher offers get filtered out as noise before they can become learning. Step Zero is the embodied, relational work that has to happen before intellectual risk-taking is even possible.

▸ Broadening attention (Odell) Coined
Sustained attention needs to be contrived -- attention is fleeting by default. "Broadening" as a practice, not a capacity: change the conditions that make sustained attention possible rather than telling students to attend differently.
